# Device Syncing

Redly offers an incredible real-time syncing engine that lets you connect devices and sync Markdown files over WebRTC.

## How it Workflow
1. From the device that has your files, open the **Sync** panel.
2. A **Sync ID** will be generated.
3. On your secondary device, type in this Sync ID.
4. Your primary device will receive a connection request. Click **Accept & Sync**.
5. Redly will handle real-time incremental syncing between the devices.
