\# Connecting Your Ideas



Turn your notes into a web of knowledge using \*\*WikiLinks\*\*.



\- \*\*Creating a link:\*\* Type `\[\[` to open a menu of all your notes. Select one to create a clickable link.

\- \*\*Backlinks:\*\* At the bottom of every note, look for the \*\*Linked References\*\* section. This shows you every other note that mentions or links to the current page, complete with context excerpts.
