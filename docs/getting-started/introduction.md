# Welcome to Redly

Redly is a private, offline-first workspace designed for people who want to own their data without sacrificing modern features. Our philosophy is simple: your notes and tasks should be **Redly available**, secure, and easy to manage.

### Why Redly?

-   **Privacy First:** We don’t have servers. Your data stays on your device.
    
-   **Offline Capability:** Work anywhere, anytime.
    
-   **Unified Workflow:** Stop switching between a note app and a task manager. Redly combines them into one seamless experience.
    
-   **Future-Proof:** All your work is stored as simple text files (Markdown), meaning you can always access your data, even without Redly.
