# Kanban Boards



Visualise your workflow by switching the Global Task view to a **Kanban Board** using `ALT + V`.



- **Organisation:** Tasks move into columns based on their last `#tag`.

- **Custom Columns:** Want a "Review" column? Just add `#review` to a task.

- **Drag \& Drop:** Move cards between columns. Redly will automatically update the text in your note to match the new status.
